Deck Allocator for Archidekt

What counts as "in the deck box"

Cards whose first category is ticked here don't use a physical copy. Theorycrafted decks never use copies.

Features

Search results are swapped to your free non-foil copy before Archidekt shows them, so adding the card adds your printing.

Data

New cards are picked up on every page change with 1–3 requests; the full reload (≈35 requests for 3,500 records) also catches deletions and quantity changes.
